Research

Published · NHSJS, April 2026Gold CREST Award

Fairness and Dependency Preservation in Synthetic Credit Data

Can synthetic data safely stand in for real data in loan approval models? I compared SMOTE, CTGAN, TVAE and Gaussian Copula on the German Credit dataset and created a Dependency Preservation Score to measure how well each kept the relationships between credit variables intact.

The finding: every synthetic method distorted those relationships and made fairness less stable, so for high-stakes decisions like lending, original data with fairness-aware preprocessing is the safer choice. The work earned a Gold CREST Award from the British Science Association.

S.T. Yau Awards · Computer Science

Emotions at the Gate: Investigating Emotion-Specific Neuron Clustering in a Large Language Model

I built a dataset of about 16,600 Chinese-language texts labelled across six emotions, then looked inside Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, capturing its gating activations and residual stream, and tested for emotion-specific neuron patterns with chi-square, permutation and z-tests plus clustering.

Anger, disgust, fear and sadness each light up a small, consistent set of neurons, almost like dedicated wiring. Happiness and surprise use more flexible, context-dependent pathways, and the overlaps partly mirror how psychologists group emotions. Understanding this could help build safer, more emotionally aware AI.

IB Extended Essay

Do reinforcement-learning pricing algorithms raise the risk of tacit collusion?

I built an original Q-learning simulation of Indian ride-hailing and food-delivery platforms (Ola and Uber, Rapido and Namma Yatri, Zomato and Swiggy), using Bangalore and Mumbai as contrasting markets. More competition sharply cut collusion risk: the simulated index fell from 0.73 in an Ola–Uber duopoly to 0.20 with three players or strong outside options.

AgriBridge

A multilingual Android app for Indian farmers earning under ₹10,000 a month. It helps them find nearby markets, government schemes and credit information in their own language, with a chatbot for quick questions.

I validated it with NABARD and three Farmer Producer Organisations in Karnataka, raised ₹1,20,000 to build it, and early results suggest around a 10% rise in income. I presented it at the 64th UN Commission for Social Development in New York as a 1M1B Changemaker.
