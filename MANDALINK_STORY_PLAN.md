# Mandalink story mode: 猴王闹海岛 The Monkey King Crashes the Islands

Status: **draft for approval.** No game code changes until this is signed off.

A standalone, loud, funny side story in Mandalink world. Seven chapters (called 回, like the chapters of *Journey to the West*), one boss each, Chinese on every surface. It teaches lightly: about 25 everyday words picked up through play, never through a quiz screen. It has its own save and never touches the Multiverse Saga.

*Journey to the West* is a 16th-century classic and in the public domain. Every character here is drawn and written fresh for this site, not copied from any film, show or game version.

---

## 1. The premise

**孙悟空 Sūn Wùkōng**, the Monkey King, the self-proclaimed 齐天大圣 (Great Sage Equal to Heaven), is **bored**. He's been stuck in a book for 400 years. Then he smells something new: a world built entirely out of radicals.

He crashes in on his cloud, yells **"俺老孙来也！"** ("Old Sun is here!"), grabs every radical he can carry, and bounces off across the islands, leaving monkeys, chaos and peach pits everywhere.

You chase him from island to island, beat the trouble he stirs up, and get the radicals back. At the end he admits the truth: he wasn't stealing them to be evil. He just wanted someone who could **keep up with him**.

---

## 2. Chinese all over

- **Chinese first, everywhere.** Signs, chapter cards, boss names, menus and dialogue are all in Chinese. Under every line: pinyin. Tap or hover for English.
- **The HUD speaks Chinese too:** 跳 on the jump button, 开始 to start, 继续 to continue, 胜利！ when you win, 再来！ when you lose.
- **Wukong's catchphrases** come back again and again until players know them: 俺老孙来也！ · 妖怪，哪里跑！ ("Demon, where do you think you're running?") · 好玩！ ("Fun!") · 无聊… ("Boring…")
- **Decor:** red lanterns with 福, firecracker strings that pop when you pass, banners, peaches, clouds with swirls.

Each word a player *needs* (to open a gate, cast a move or read a clue) appears at least three times, so they learn it without being taught.

## 3. Your moves (unlocked as you play)

| Move | Unlocked | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **金箍棒** the staff | 第一回 | Your weapon. Tap to swing, hold to make it **grow** and slam bosses from far away. |
| **筋斗云** the cloud | 第二回 | Ride a little cloud across gaps between islands. Fully unlocked by the end: fly anywhere in Mandalink world. |
| **分身** clones | 第四回 | Blow on a hair to spawn 3 decoy clones that distract bosses. |
| **火眼金睛** fire eyes | 第五回 | See through disguises and hidden things for a few seconds. |
| **Character casting** | 第七回 | Combine radicals from your tray to cast characters as attacks. |

## 4. The chapters (回)

### 第一回 齐天大圣来了！ The Great Sage Has Arrived (the hub)
- **What happens:** Wukong crashes onto the hub, steals the radicals off the monuments, and leaves a dozen cheeky monkeys behind. You grab the **金箍棒** he drops (it's a decoy, but it still works).
- **Fun bit:** monkey round-up. Chase down monkeys hiding in trees, on the shrine and under the tablet; each one squeaks a word as you catch it.
- **Boss: 猴子军团 The Monkey Army.** Waves of monkeys throw peaches and banana peels. *Rule:* the peels make you slide, so stand still to dodge and swing when they bunch up.
- **Words:** 猴 (monkey) · 大 (big) · 小 (small) · 好玩 (fun)
- **Hook:** Wukong shouts from the 氵 island: "抓不到我！" ("Can't catch me!") and dives into the waterfall.

### 第二回 水帘洞 The Water Curtain Cave (氵 island)
- **What happens:** behind the waterfall is Wukong's cave. Inside, a passage leads down to the **东海龙宫**, the Dragon King's underwater palace, where Wukong is "borrowing" the radical 氵.
- **Fun bit:** an underwater chase on the **筋斗云** (unlocked here) through rings of bubbles and jellyfish.
- **Boss: 东海龙王 The Dragon King of the East Sea.** A huge blue water dragon. *Rule:* he sucks in water before each attack, so slam his open mouth with the growing staff.
- **Words:** 水 (water) · 河 (river) · 海 (sea) · 龙 (dragon)
- **Hook:** Wukong sneezes from across the sky. He's in the peach orchard, and the peaches are already disappearing.

### 第三回 蟠桃园 The Peach Orchard (木 island)
- **What happens:** the 木 island's trees are now Heaven's peach orchard, and Wukong is eating everything. Peach pits rain from the canopy.
- **Fun bit:** peach catch. Catch falling peaches in a basket. Golden peaches are worth more, and rotten ones make you burp a random character.
- **Boss: 巨灵神 The Giant Spirit.** A gigantic heavenly guard with an axe, sent to catch Wukong but chasing *you* instead. *Rule:* he can only see what's moving, so freeze when his eye glows, then hit him from behind.
- **Words:** 木 (tree) · 林 (forest) · 桃 (peach) · 吃 (to eat)
- **Hook:** a tree sprouts a tail. That's no tree, that's Wukong in disguise. He giggles and vanishes.

### 第四回 七十二变 The 72 Transformations (亻 island)
- **What happens:** Wukong is hiding on the 亻 island by transforming into villagers, lanterns, a bench, even a whole temple. In the original story, his tail gives him away as a flagpole behind the temple. Here, *you* have to spot it.
- **Fun bit:** "Which one is him?" Ask villagers questions in simple Chinese (你是谁？ "Who are you?"). Wukong always answers wrong in a funny way.
- **Boss: 二郎神 Erlang Shen and his dog 哮天犬.** The three-eyed god Wukong famously fought. *Rule:* when his third eye opens, it reveals your clones, so use your new **分身** clones between blinks.
- **Words:** 人 (person) · 你 (you) · 我 (me) · 谁 (who)
- **Hook:** Erlang Shen's heavenly team traps Wukong and throws him in a furnace. Then the furnace starts chasing *you*.

### 第五回 八卦炉 The Eight-Trigram Furnace (日月 island)
- **What happens:** the furnace where Wukong was famously cooked for 49 days (and came out with burning golden eyes) now rolls around the 日月 island, spitting fire, with Wukong still inside and very annoyed.
- **Fun bit:** the sun and moon have been knocked out of the sky. Put them back to change day into night and cool the furnace down.
- **Boss: 八卦炉 The Furnace itself.** A giant bronze cauldron on legs. *Rule:* its fire follows the trigram markings on the floor, so learn the pattern, then crack the lid. Out pops Wukong, and you gain **火眼金睛** fire eyes.
- **Words:** 火 (fire) · 日 (sun) · 月 (moon) · 明 (bright)
- **Hook:** Wukong cartwheels off toward the sky: "大闹天宫！" ("Havoc in Heaven!")

### 第六回 南天门 The Southern Gate of Heaven (口 island)
- **What happens:** the 口 island is now Heaven's front gate, and the guards only open up for the right **tones**. Wukong is somewhere inside, causing havoc.
- **Fun bit:** the tone gates. Guards shout a word, you pick the tone, and the gate swings open (mā má mǎ mà, played out loud by the browser's Chinese voice). Get it wrong and a guard hits you with a pillow.
- **Boss: 哪吒 Nezha.** The fierce boy-god on fire wheels, with a spear and a ring he throws. *Rule:* his wheels leave fire trails, so lure him into running over his own trail and use **火眼金睛** to spot which ring is real.
- **Words:** 口 (mouth) · 天 (heaven, sky) · 门 (gate, door) and the four tones
- **Hook:** Wukong sits on the gate laughing: "你很厉害！最后一次！" ("You're good! One last time!") A new island rises on a cloud.

### 第七回 筋斗云对决 The Cloud Duel (a new cloud island)
- **What happens:** the final showdown, on a cloud island high above all the others.
- **Phase 1, the race:** a cloud race around every island in Mandalink world. Wukong cheats constantly.
- **Phase 2, the duel:** staff against staff, clones against clones.
- **Phase 3, the trick:** to win, you have to **out-trick the trickster**. Combine radicals to cast characters he's never seen: 日 + 月 = 明 blinds him, and 木 + 木 = 林 traps him in a forest.
- **The ending:** Wukong flops onto the cloud laughing, gives back every radical, and admits he was just lonely in his book. You teach him to say **你好** properly, and he decides to stay on the islands. From now on he's there to race whenever you visit.
- **Words:** 云 (cloud) · 你好 (hello) · 朋友 (friend)

### 尾声 Epilogue
- Wukong's cloud is now yours: **fly freely** anywhere in Mandalink world.
- He hangs out on the hub and challenges you to rematches of any chapter.
- The quest board unlocks a code for an exclusive 🐒 **齐天大圣 Great Sage** badge on Mandalink.

## 6. How it fits into the site

- **Standalone,** with its own save (`ml-story`), own chapter list and own goal panel styled like an old book page with red seals. It never touches the Multiverse Saga.
- **The entry point:** Wukong's cloud is parked by the Mandalink tablet with a sign: **"齐天大圣到此一游"** ("The Great Sage was here"). That's a joke from the novel, where he scrawls it on what he thinks is the edge of the universe. Click it to start.
- **Bosses** reuse the existing arena engine, with a new pattern and the staff/cloud/clone moves added in.
- **Skipping:** the same skip-twice confirmation as the main saga.
- **The badge:** one more quest code on the quest board and in Mandalink's quest badges. No new backend work.

## 8. Update: everything happens in the world, and Part Two

**In the world, not in a pop-up.** When a chapter starts, an arena rises out of the sea next to that chapter's island (第一回 and 第十回 use one west of the hub, 第七回 uses the cloud island). You walk onto it and play every mini-game and boss as yourself, in third person, with the normal controls (WASD or the joystick, jump, the cloud). The dialogue and word cards still use the overlay between rounds. When the chapter ends, the arena sinks again.

- 抓小猴: monkeys pop out of holes in the arena. Run into them, or tap them.
- 接桃子 / 接水滴: things fall from the sky with a shadow underneath, and you stand under them with a basket on your head.
- 坐云去追 / 云上赛跑: you're on the cloud, flying through bubble rings or racing Wukong to the stars.
- 你是谁？: eight props in a circle. The monkey tail points into the middle, so you have to go in and look around.
- 听声调: four gates with the four tones. Walk through the right one.
- Bosses stand on the far side of the arena. Your peaches fly by themselves. You dodge in 3D (you can jump some attacks), and the move buttons (变大, 分身, 火眼) sit at the bottom.

**第二部 · 西天路上 (Part Two, chapters 8–10).** It starts after the Cloud Duel. The quest badge still unlocks after chapter 7, so nobody loses theirs.

- **第八回 三打白骨精** (女 island): spot the White Bone Demon in disguise with 火眼, then fight her. She turns invisible and sends bone bats.
- **第九回 火焰山** (日月 island): fan out spreading fires with the Earth God's little fan, then fight Princess Iron Fan. Hide behind rocks when she blows.
- **第十回 牛魔王** (west of the hub): grab the characters with the 牛/牜 radical while calves charge you, then fight the Bull Demon King. Make him ram a rock so he gets dizzy, and jump his stomp rings.

**宝贝 treasures** (the 🎒 button in Mandalink world). These unlock as you finish chapters, and old saves get theirs straight away.

- **Looks:** 金箍, 虎皮裙, 混天绫, 凤翅紫金冠, 袈裟, 牛角
- **Pets that follow you:** 小猴, 小白龙, 哮天犬, 小牛
- **Magic weapons (extra boss buttons):** 乾坤圈 (stuns), 芭蕉扇 (blows away everything flying at you)
- **Replay:** any chapter you've finished

Your choices are saved in `ml-story` as `look` and `pet`.

## 9. Update: a bigger Mandalink world (云路, the Cloud Road)

- **The ring road:** 云路 runs all the way around the world, past the recipe islands. Golden paths lead out to it from five of the radical islands, and the Hall of Fame bridge joins it on the 氵 side. Everything is walkable, so you don't need the cloud.
- **Six new islands on the ring.** Each has someone to talk to: walk up to them or tap them for Chinese, pinyin, and English on tap.
  - 茶园 the Tea Garden: a tea farmer explains 茶 (the grass radical, with a person and a tree underneath)
  - 竹林 the Bamboo Grove: a panda
  - 灯笼市场 the Lantern Market: a stallholder and "多少钱？"
  - 龙门 the Dragon Gate: an old carp, a koi pond and a waterfall
  - 风筝草地 the Kite Meadow: a child flying kites
  - 书院 the Study Hall: a teacher, and a word wall of every word you've learned with Wukong (tap it to hear them)
- **12 字宝石 character gems,** two hidden on each new island. They're saved in this browser as `ml-gems`, and finding all 12 unlocks the 玉佩 jade pendant look in 🎒.
- **A 地图 map board** on the hub, next to the tablet.
- **Far-off floating islands and waterfalls** for depth.
- **No clashes:** the chapter arenas, recipe islands and the Hall of Fame keep their places; the new ring sits outside all of them.
