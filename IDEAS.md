# Ideas for later

A backlog of feature ideas for rayanupadhyay.com, saved so they aren't lost. Nothing here is planned or promised yet.

## Still on the main plan
- The four Meridian General action games: nano-ship vessel race, defib rhythm fight, hover-gurney ER rush, slow-mo laser surgery.
- Multiverse Hub in the lair (replacing the old gate areas), The Final Draft, and the "Thanks for exploring" ending room.
- A cooled Celestial-core shard as System Failure's trace in space.

## Look and atmosphere
- A dark grade for the whole site: per-world colour grading, film grain, vignette, wet reflective streets, light shafts, flickering lights, lightning, and a Low/High/Auto quality setting.
- The city decays as the story gets darker (smoke, ash, broken windows, curfew siren) and heals after Page One.
- A torch for dark places, with secrets only visible in torchlight.
- A horror-tinged side area (an abandoned Broker lab under the dam).
- Seasons from the real date: Diwali, monsoon, Chinese New Year lanterns, birthday snow.
- Weather that crosses worlds (rain in Bangalore, mist in Mandalink, wet hospital windows).
- Time-of-day events (a night market after 9 pm, something at midnight).
- An underwater world.

## Gameplay
- Photo mode, and a photo-booth sticker set.
- Boss rematches at the trophy-hall statues; a boss rush tower.
- New Game+ for the saga; a daily challenge with a streak.
- A boss-maker with share codes; ghost races against your best runs.
- A heist chapter (stealth and planning in Broker Tower).
- A villain's-eye chapter; a "what if" branch at a big choice.
- Co-op through a share link (the MQTT multiplayer code already exists).
- A city builder for rebuilding damaged Bangalore blocks.
- Mandalink spell combat as a rare power-up in other worlds.
- A "diagnose it" ward in Meridian General (visor scanning, not a quiz).
- A Bangalore gully cricket mini-game.
- Fishing in Mandalink (fish with radicals on their scales).
- Street food stalls with small buffs (chai, dosa, vada pav).
- A telescope event that starts a space side-mission.
- A companion (a drone or a stray dog) that follows you between worlds; a dog on your street you can pet and name.
- A speedrun timer with splits; gamepad support with rumble.

## Story and characters
- Side stories for the cast (Ama's first shift, Reyes's comeback, Kai's day, Wukong and a phone).
- Letters from characters in a mailbox in your room.
- A "where are they now" board.
- An in-game phone feed of character posts.
- Collectible cassette tapes with lore logs.
- "Truth glasses" that reveal a hidden layer across every world.
- A newspaper archive of every story's front page.

## The real-life room
- A visitor guestbook wall (needs a small backend).
- "Rayan's day" mode from a status.json you edit.
- Achievements that unlock real things (wallpapers, write-ups, a "how I built this" tour).
- A hidden developer room of early versions and scrapped ideas.
- The room filling up with trophies as stories are finished.
- A credits jukebox in the lair.
- A world map on the room's map table for fast travel.
- Spotify: an embedded player for everyone, and optional "connect your own Spotify" (needs a developer app, Premium, and a 25-user allowlist).

## Accessibility
- Subtitles for sounds, a colourblind-friendly palette, hold-to-attack, adjustable text size.
