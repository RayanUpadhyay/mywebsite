# Season plans

Plans for seasons that aren't built yet. Each season is four weeks with a chapter a week, its own original villain, a tool or vehicle you keep, three skins (story, challenge, villain) and a permanent change to the map. See the Seasons section of `CLAUDE.md` for how a season file plugs into the page.

## High Water (saved for a future season)

**Premise.** Something comes through the tear that opened over Crash Isle at the end of Season 1, and the sea starts to rise. If this season doesn't directly follow Landfall, the opening needs a new reason for the flood.

**Villain (original): the Undertow.** A tall figure made of moving seawater, with a crown of driftwood and a lantern of deep-sea light. It wants to pull Bangalore down into its own flooded world. It talks like the tide: slow, patient, always returning.

**Characters.**
- The Keeper comes back.
- New ally: a ferry pilot who knows every channel once the streets flood.

**Kept for good: the skiff.** A small jet boat. It drives like the cars, but on water: through flooded streets, around the island and out to the other lands.

**Chapters.**

| Week | Chapter | What you do | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rising | The low streets flood overnight. Get the skiff, rescue people stranded on rooftops, and shut the first floodgate. | **Bilge**, a crab-like construct of wreckage that rams boats. You fight it from the skiff. |
| 2 | Undercurrent | Dive into the drowned blocks to find the source: underwater swimming with a breath meter and air pockets. | **The Pearl Warden**, an eel-like guardian that circles you underwater. Hit its glowing pearls while dodging currents. |
| 3 | The Floodgate | Hold the new west-coast sea wall against waves of drowned constructs while the gates close. | **The Tide Engine**, a pump-monster in the wall. Its tide rises and falls, changing where you can stand. |
| 4 | High Water (finale) | Face the Undertow. | Three phases:<br>1. On the skiff, dodge whirlpools and shoot its lantern.<br>2. On a platform that sinks as the tide rises.<br>3. Up close. |

**Weapons.**
- Tide cannon: pushes enemies back.
- Anchor flail: a heavy melee weapon with a wide spin.
- Maybe a net launcher that slows enemies.

**Skins.**
- Story skin, Floodrunner: finish chapters 1–3.
- Challenge skin, Abyssal: beat the Pearl Warden without surfacing for air.
- Villain skin, Undertow's Crown: beat the finale.

**Left on the map.** A canal district, where one low neighbourhood stays as water streets you can boat through, and a sea wall on the west coast.

**Winter crossover.** If the season runs in December: floodwater freezes at night, the canals become skating lanes, and the skiff gets a light on the bow.

**Engine work it needs** (in `index.html`, so later seasons can use it too):
- boat physics for the skiff;
- an underwater mode (swimming down, a breath meter, blue fog, air pockets);
- a water level that can rise and fall.
