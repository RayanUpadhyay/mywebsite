# Season plans

The plan for all 15 seasons, plus ideas saved for later. Each season is four weeks with a chapter a week, starting 18 November 2026 and cycling. Every season has its own original villain, an intro cinematic, weekly chapters and a finale. It gives you a tool or vehicle you keep, three skins and a permanent change to the map. See the Seasons section of `CLAUDE.md` for how a season file plugs into the page.

## The rules that hold for every season

- The seasons are loosely linked: each has its own story and you can jump in at any one. Light threads run through them all: the Keeper (a mysterious figure), the traces each season leaves on the map, and the tear in the sky.
- Every season changes the map, gives you one new tool or vehicle to keep, and leaves a permanent trace.
- Progress is saved in `ru-season` and your name in `ru-heroname` (default "Traveller").
- Real winter (December to early January) is a bonus layer on top of whichever season is running.
- All villains, aliens and skins are original: no Marvel or DC characters. Season 7's aliens are clearly fictional, with no real-world conspiracy theories.
- **Skins:**
  - There are three per season (45 in all):
    - a story skin (finish the story);
    - a challenge skin (complete the season's challenge);
    - a villain skin (beat the villain).
  - They are earned by playing, never bought, and kept for good (`ru-arsenal`).
  - You equip them from the Wardrobe on the phone.
  - A season currency can buy small extras such as colour variants.
  - Owning every skin unlocks a final one: The Keeper.
- **Still to decide:**
  - whether missed skins come back when the cycle loops;
  - whether a past season's fights stay replayable.

## Season 2 · High Water (16 December 2026 – 12 January 2027)

### Story

**Cold open (16 Dec).**
- A few days after Landfall, you're visiting a friend's highrise, high up and looking out at the sea, trying to move on from everything that happened.
- The glass starts to hum. The tear over Crash Isle splits wider, and the wall of water seen at the end of Season 1 pours through it and races across the sea.
- The wave hits the tower, the window bursts, and you're thrown out into the air. Black screen.
- **You wake up on a wooden plank in the middle of a drowned Bangalore.** Only the tops of the tall buildings stick out of the water, there's wreckage everywhere, and a fin is circling.

**Villain (original): Captain Brine.** A pirate who rode the wave in through the tear on a ship built from wreckage. To him a drowned city is the perfect harbour, and he means to keep it that way. His crew raid the rooftop survivors for supplies, and the sharks follow his ship like hounds.

**Chapter 1 · Adrift (16 Dec).**
- You paddle your plank between floating wreckage while sharks circle.
- Early on you find a battered boat caught in the top of a billboard: the tool you keep for the season and after.
- You reach a camp of survivors on the rooftops, linked by rope bridges, and find your way back towards your own tower.
- Boss: **the first great shark.** It hunts you across open water until you make it to the rooftops.

**Chapter 2 · Brine's Harbour (23 Dec).**
- Brine's crew raid the rooftop camp and take its supplies.
- You chase their raider boats through the drowned streets and fight on rooftops and decks to get the supplies back.
- Boss: **Brine's first mate**, fought on a raider boat.

**Chapter 3 · The Deep (30 Dec).**
- The survivors give you diving gear, and you go down into the drowned streets: cars, bus stops and the metro line under the water.
- You find the old floodgates and pumps that could drain the city.
- Boss: **a huge armoured shark** guarding the pump house, fought underwater.

**Chapter 4 · High Water (6 Jan, finale).**
- You start the pumps and board Brine's ship as the water begins to fall.
- The fight has three phases:
  1. A boat chase.
  2. Fighting on the deck.
  3. A duel up the mast as the water drops away beneath the ship.
- **The city drains.** One neighbourhood stays underwater for good: the sunken district.
- The ending leads straight into Season 3.

### The flooded city
- While Season 2 runs, the whole city sits under about 6–8 m of water: low buildings are gone, and towers become islands.
- **The whole map is swimmable**, both on the surface and under it. Through the clear water you can see the old streets below.
- Rooftops are safe spots, linked by rope bridges and planks.
- **Sharks** patrol, and a fin shows where each one is.
  - When one locks on it circles, then lunges, with a warning you can dodge.
  - Bites hurt, and a shark can knock you out: you wake up on the nearest rooftop.
  - You stop them with harpoons: a harpoon gun you get in Chapter 1 drives them off or brings them down.
  - Getting out of the water onto a rooftop or the boat keeps you safe.
  - Bigger ones turn up in deeper water.
- **Other dangers:** jellyfish swarms, whirlpools round collapsed buildings, and debris carried by the current.
- **Christmas crossover:** at night the shallow water between rooftops freezes into skating lanes, and the boat gets a light on the bow.

### What you keep
- **Vehicle:** the boat. **Tools:** the harpoon gun and the diving gear.
- **Skins:**
  - Diver: finish the story.
  - Canal Captain: the challenge, e.g. finish Chapter 1 without being bitten.
  - Brine's Coat: beat the finale.
- **Left on the map:** the sunken district.

### Engine work (in `index.html`, so later seasons can use it too)
- Crash Isle and the other Season 1 map changes move out of `seasons/s01.js` before 16 Dec, or they vanish when Season 1 stops loading.
- **A flooded-city mode:** one big water surface over the whole map, underwater fog and light, and the traffic and pedestrians switched off. The water level can fall for the finale.
- **Swimming and diving everywhere:** reuse the sea swimming, and add diving with a breath meter.
- **A walking height on building tops**, like the mountains have.
- **Boat physics.**
- **Sharks:** new enemies that swim, circle and lunge, built on the season fight engine.

## Season 3 · The Syndicate (13 January – 9 February 2027)

### Story
While Bangalore was underwater, the police stations flooded, the phones went dead and nobody was in charge, so someone moved in. During the flood the Marigold Syndicate sold pumps, boats and "protection" to the rooftop camps. When the water drains, they own half the city. Every rebuilt shop pays them, their trucks run the streets, and their marigold stamp is on every door.

**The cast**
- **Madame Marigold:** head of the Marigold Syndicate. Elegant and polite, always with a fresh marigold in her lapel, and never seen getting her hands dirty. *“A city this beautiful needs someone to look after it.”*
- **Her three lieutenants:**
  - Brass, the enforcer;
  - the Ledger, the accountant;
  - Velvet, the getaway driver.
- **Inspector Devi:** an honest police inspector whose station is still full of mud. Officially she has no power left, so she works with you. Or so it seems.
- **The Keeper:** in the background. The Syndicate is looking for something that came through the tear.

**Cold open (13 Jan).**
- The morning after the drain, the city is mud and wreckage, and people are trying to reopen.
- You walk down your street and every shutter has a fresh marigold stamp.
- A collector in a cream suit tells a chaiwala his "protection" is due, and when he can't pay his cart gets tipped over.
- Inspector Devi pulls up in a mud-caked police jeep: *“My station is underwater and my phone is dead. You’re the only one in this city I’ve seen do anything. Want a job?”*

**Chapters**

| Week | Chapter | What you do | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · 13 Jan | Protection | Your street is paying protection. Help the stall owners stand up to the collectors and free your district. You learn to slide and vault in a chase after a collector through the market. The first alley ambushes, and the Syndicate raids your safehouse while you sleep. | **Brass**, a big brawler with knuckle-dusters, fought in the market. |
| 2 · 20 Jan | The Ledger | Steal the Syndicate's ledger (who pays, and who's on the payroll) in a heist on their counting house. Quiet is optional: disguises, timing and sliding under closing shutters, or go in loud. Then **the fight you lose** (below). | **The Ledger**, who fights with traps, alarms, locking doors and flying safes. He turns out to be **Mr. Ravindran**, the city's old tax inspector, whom everyone thought drowned in the flood. His face is still on the missing-persons posters on every wall. |
| 3 · 27 Jan | The Velvet Run | Get the ledger back. You get the rope swing and chase the smuggling convoys roof to roof and in a getaway car of your own. One convoy is a decoy, and it's an ambush. | **Velvet**, in a high-speed chase on the highway. Beaten, she switches sides and tells you about the missing payroll page. |
| 4 · 3 Feb | Marigold (finale) | Storm Marigold's tower with Inspector Devi: a swing route up the outside instead of the stairs, with snipers on the rooftops round it. | Three phases:<br>1. A gunfight in the lobby.<br>2. A rooftop chase.<br>3. Marigold in her greenhouse penthouse. |

**The fight you lose (end of Chapter 2).**
- You escape the counting house with the ledger and run straight into a trap: Brass and Velvet together. Then Madame Marigold steps out of the car.
- The fight can't be won. Brass's hits get stronger every round, and when you're on your last heart it stops.
- Marigold takes the ledger and pins a marigold on your jacket: *“Wear it. Now you belong to me too.”*
- You wake up in Devi's jeep. She *found* you, of course, and the ledger is gone.

**Plot twists.**
1. **The Ledger** is Mr. Ravindran, the tax inspector everyone thought had drowned. He faked his death to keep the Syndicate's books.
2. **Velvet switches sides** after Chapter 3. She tells you a payroll page is missing: the strongest hint at Devi, which most players will still miss.
3. **The tear connection:** Marigold's greenhouse is full of plants that came through the tear, and she's been selling them.
4. **Devi** in the finale.

**Devi's betrayal is only hinted at, never stated.**
- She always arrives just after the Syndicate has cleared out.
- She asks for the ledger "for evidence", and the page with the payroll is missing when it comes back.
- One collector salutes her by mistake, then pretends he was waving.
- Her jeep is the only clean car in the city.

**The finale (sets up Season 4).**
1. You beat Marigold in her greenhouse penthouse. She backs out onto the terrace, against the railing, bleeding marigold petals from a torn lapel.
2. As you go to arrest her, she looks past you at Devi and laughs. Her last words:
   *“You’re arresting the wrong woman, {N}. She paid for every stamp on every door. She’ll make you miss me.”*
3. Devi steps forward: *“Enough.”* She shoves Marigold over the railing.
4. **Marigold's fall:**
   - the camera follows her down past the glass floors of her own tower, petals streaming up behind her;
   - it cuts to the petals settling on the street before she lands.
5. **Devi runs off.** She grabs the ledger and sprints for the stairwell, and you chase her down two floors. She stops on the landing just long enough to say *“You cleaned up this city for me. Thank you.”* Then she's gone.
6. Every light in Bangalore goes out, leading straight into **Season 4 · Blackout**, with Devi as its villain.

### How it plays
- **Territory map:**
  - five districts start under the Syndicate, and the phone map shows which;
  - you free a district by doing three of its four rackets: stop a collection, smash a collection point, beat its crew, rescue a shopkeeper;
  - freed districts change for good: the stamps come off the doors, stalls reopen and music comes back.
- **Heat:**
  - causing trouble raises the Syndicate's attention, shown as one to three marigolds on screen;
  - at high heat, goon cars hunt you and roadblocks go up;
  - you lose them by lying low or reaching a safehouse;
  - this reuses the existing police-chase code with Syndicate cars.
- **Heists:**
  - each is an interior level, like the garage;
  - the quiet way is optional: disguises, guard sight-cones (like the Season 1 searchlights), timing and crouch takedowns;
  - the loud way means alarms, lockdown doors and safe traps;
  - the challenge skin rewards doing the Ledger heist unseen.
- **Ambushes:**
  - at high heat, goons jump out of alleys and doorways;
  - a van screeches up and its side door slides open;
  - a rooftop sniper's red laser dot gives you about a second and a half to dodge or take cover;
  - some "chaiwalas" and auto drivers are Syndicate plants who swing at you as you pass (the tell is a marigold pinned somewhere on them);
  - there's one scripted ambush per chapter.
- **New ways to move** (the movement code is frozen, so like the flood's swimming these take over after normal movement):
  - **Rope swing:** the season's tool, a wrist launcher you keep. It fires a line to a rooftop ledge and swings you across a street: hold to swing, let go to fly. Roof to roof only, and phones get a Swing button. Season 9's grappling hook is for climbing instead.
  - **Slide:** sprint, then crouch, to slide under closing shutters, through gaps and under sniper lines. A slide into an enemy knocks them down.
  - **Vault:** sprint at a low wall or a car bonnet to go over it.
  - **Drop takedown:** swing or jump down onto a goon from above.
- **Car combat:** chases, ramming, tyre spikes and the Comet's nitro.
- **Weapons:** the Season 1 guns and blades stay, plus the season's gadgets.
- **Tone:**
  - fights stay cartoon-style: enemies are knocked out, there's no gore, and the street crews break into a daze, not blood;
  - Marigold's fall is shown, but the scene cuts away before she lands.

### What you keep
- **Vehicle:** the Comet, a souped-up getaway car with nitro, parked in your garage afterwards.
- **Tool:** the rope swing (wrist launcher).
- **Gadgets:**
  - stun baton (melee);
  - tyre-spike launcher (for chases);
  - flashbangs (for heists).
- **Skins:**
  - Undercover: finish the story;
  - Clean Hands: do the Ledger heist without being seen;
  - Marigold's Suit: beat the finale.
- **Left on the map:** Marigold's tower, now Devi's, with its lights off, and marigold graffiti across the city.

### The trailer (a third look, different from Seasons 1 and 2)
- **Look:** noir. Desaturated black and white, with only marigold orange left in colour: the stamps, Marigold's flower, and the brake lights in the chase.
- **Type:** 1970s crime film, with a condensed typewriter or slab-serif and title cards like case-file stamps (“CASE NO. 3 · THE SYNDICATE”).
- **Score:** walking upright bass, brushed snare, a muted trumpet and a tense string stab on each cut, building into a car-chase beat.
- **Ending:** the betrayal stays secret. The only hint is one line from Devi: *“Trust me.”*

## Season 4 · Blackout (10 February – 9 March 2027)

### Story
Bangalore has been dark since the night Marigold fell. Devi has made herself **Acting Commissioner**, declared a state of emergency and put the city under curfew. She holds the only working generators, and she sells power the way Marigold sold protection. To most people she's the woman holding the city together. To you she's a murderer, and she's made sure the city thinks the murderer is **you**.

**The cast**
- **Inspector (now Commissioner) Devi:** the villain. She's calm and reasonable on every radio broadcast, and ruthless everywhere else. *“A city in the dark needs one light to follow.”*
- **Static:** her saboteur. He lives in the power lines, blacks out a district the moment it's fixed, and fights with arcs of electricity.
- **Velvet:** the ally since Season 3. She runs smuggled power cells to the people Devi cuts off.
- **Brass:** he survived Season 3, and now he's Devi's muscle.
- **The Keeper:** in the background. Something under the sea to the west is getting warmer.

**Cold open (10 Feb).**
- The city is pitch-black at night, with only candles in windows and police searchlights sweeping the streets.
- A giant screen on Devi's tower flickers on, running on her generators: *“Citizens: the person responsible for Madame Marigold’s death is still at large.”*
- Then your face fills the screen. WANTED.
- Velvet's van skids up beside you: *“Get in. Now.”*

**Chapters**

| Week | Chapter | What you do | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · 10 Feb | Lights Out | On the run, framed. Get the lantern and the toolkit from Velvet's hideout, and repair your district's substation in a wire-matching mini-game while Devi's patrols sweep the streets. The lights come back on in one district, for a minute, until Static blacks it out again. | **Static**, in an ambush on the substation: dodge his arcs, and fight with the lantern lit so you can see him move along the wires. |
| 2 · 17 Feb | Curfew | Devi's curfew. Run Velvet's power cells to the hospitals and the rooftop camps, past patrol cars, drone searchlights and checkpoints, swinging across dark streets. People start to help you, and your wanted poster gets torn down in the districts you help. | **Brass, the rematch:** this time you win the fight you lost in Season 3. |
| 3 · 24 Feb | Live Wire | Break into the old power station, Static's lair, and fight through the turbine hall in the dark. Beaten, Static tells you the truth: Devi isn't saving the city's power, she's draining it into a rig out at sea, drilling into the hot vent under the seabed west of Crash Isle. | **Static** in the turbine hall: live floors, a turbine you shut down to ground him, and a final chase along the high-voltage lines. |
| 4 · 3 Mar | Overload (finale) | Take the boat out to Devi's drilling rig at night, in a storm. She's overloading the vent to power a city that only she controls. | Three phases:<br>1. The rig deck, dark except for your lantern and the lightning.<br>2. The control room, as she overloads the drill and the rig starts to shake.<br>3. Devi in a stolen powered work suit on the drill platform, as the sea starts to boil. |

**The finale (sets up Season 5).**
1. You beat Devi on the drill platform. The overload can't be stopped, and the drill punches through into the vent.
2. The seabed cracks. Below the rig, the sea glows orange.
3. Devi clings to the platform as it tips towards the glow. Her last words: *“I only wanted the lights to stay on… for me.”*
4. The platform gives way and she falls into the rising lava. The camera holds on her silhouette as she sinks into the glow and melts away into it. No gore: it fades into the light.
5. You get to the boat as the rig collapses.
6. Behind you, **a volcano rises out of the sea**, steam and fire pouring up into the sky.
7. Across Bangalore every light comes back on at once, because the city's new power plant is finally free of Devi's control.
8. **Last shot:** on the beach, a row of figures in ash-grey robes kneels facing the volcano. One of them lowers her hood. **Season 5 · The Eruption.**

### How it plays
- **Darkness:**
  - nights are much darker, with street lights out across districts you haven't fixed;
  - your lantern lights the way, but it also makes you easier to spot;
  - it's the season's tool, and you keep it.
- **Wanted level:** Devi's police are hunting you. Patrol cars, drone searchlights (like Season 1's sentries) and checkpoints, with disguises and the rope swing to slip past.
- **Fixing the grid:**
  - each district has a substation, with a quick wire-and-fuse puzzle from the toolkit;
  - a district you fix lights up and stays lit, and its people start to help you;
  - Static comes back to black out fixed districts, so you have to defend them.
- **Electric fights:**
  - live puddles and wires you mustn't stand on;
  - arcs you can see coming;
  - Static moves along the cables, so you cut or ground them.
- **Everything from Seasons 1–3 carries over:** the guns and blades, the rope swing, the slide and vault, and the Comet.

### What you keep
- **Tools:** the lantern and the toolkit.
- **Skins:**
  - Lineworker: finish the story;
  - Lantern Bearer: the challenge, e.g. fix every district's substation;
  - Static: beat him in Chapter 3.
- **Left on the map:** the new power plant, and the city lit again. Offshore, the new volcano that becomes Season 5's Ember Island.

### The trailer
- **Look:** near-total darkness lit only by torches, searchlights, sparks and lightning, with electric blue as the only strong colour.
- **Type:** a flickering neon or LED dot-matrix display, like a failing signboard.
- **Score:** buzzing mains hum, relay clicks, sparks and a heartbeat, with a synth pulse that drops out in each blackout. At the end it gives way to a deep volcanic rumble.

## The Moon (saved for a future season)

**Premise.** The meteor came from the Moon. Something living inside it sends out keys like the meteor, and wherever one lands it opens a door and takes something back. This game's Moon is its own place, with air pockets, ruins and someone living inside it.

**Villain (original): the Archivist.** An ancient collector, tall and thin, with a coat of hanging lanterns. It sleeps in a vault deep inside the Moon, and each lantern holds a piece of a world it has taken.

**Kept for good.**
- A rocket, built on Crash Isle. Its launch pad stays on the map.
- A moon rover, a buggy with big, bouncy low-gravity jumps. You can bring it back and drive it in Bangalore.

**Chapters.**

| Chapter | What you do | Boss |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Liftoff | Build the rocket on Crash Isle from scrap collected around the city. Launch cinematic, then a rough landing near the Moon's north pole, with Earth in the sky. | **The Welcome Party**: moon drones swarm the landing site. |
| 2 · Tranquility Base | An abandoned moonbase, with low gravity, an oxygen meter (refills at airlocks and tanks) and crew logs. | **The Custodian**, a robot that switches the base's gravity on and off. |
| 3 · The Dark Side | Drive the rover across the far side: glowing craters, chasms to jump, and diggers boring into the core. | **The Burrower**, a drilling machine that tunnels under you and bursts out. |
| 4 · Heart of the Moon | The vault inside the Moon, full of things taken from other worlds. | Three phases:<br>1. A rover chase down the spiral tunnel.<br>2. Zero gravity in the hollow core.<br>3. Close up as the vault collapses.<br>You escape on the rocket. |

**After the finale.** The Moon is left cracked, and stays that way in Bangalore's sky.

**Weapons.**
- Plasma lance: long reach.
- Gravity gun: pulls or pushes enemies and rocks.
- Star shot: hold to charge.

**Skins.** Moonwalker (story), Zero-G (beat the Custodian without touching the floor while gravity is off), Archivist's Lanterns (villain).

**Engine work.** A separate Moon world in `index.html` (the old world system is frozen Mandalink code), low and zero gravity, an oxygen meter, rocket launch and landing scenes, and the rover.
