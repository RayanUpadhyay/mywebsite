# Story mode remake: plan

Status: **draft for approval.** No game code changes until this is signed off.

One connected story, 15 chapters in 4 acts. Every villain, clue and mechanic traces back to a single deal made before chapter 1.

---

## 1. The premise

Years before chapter 1, **Victor von Doom** and **the Broker** sign a deal.

- **The Broker** runs Broker Dynamics' *Continuity Programme*. It grows people from one template, **Patient Zero**, a man locked in a vault under the Broker's tower. Each growth run is a *batch*; each body is a *tank*.
- **Doom** funds it. He wants every version of one particular hero, harvested from every Earth, so he can find the version that *can't be copied*. That version is the only thing in the multiverse that could stop him.
- **{N}** is **tank four** of batch one. The Broker gave him a flat, a street, a bedroom and a Batman figure on a shelf, then watched. {N} doesn't know.

The lair comic that "tells the story" is the programme's surveillance log, drawn as a comic so a child-shaped subject would keep it.

## 3. The chapters

Numbers are the new chapter numbers. Each entry gives the setting, goal steps, boss rule, clues, choice, world changes, and the hook into the next chapter.

### Act 1: The Multiverse

**1. Your Room** (room → lair)
- **Steps:**
  1. Tilt the Batman figure; the lair opens.
  2. Investigation (3 items): an empty baby-photo frame on the shelf; the pod label *"BD-CP / BATCH 0 / SYNTHETIC"*; the comic stand.
  3. Take a weapon.
  4. Open the Synthetic's pod and beat it.
- **Boss rule, the Synthetic:** it copies your *last* attack. Hit it twice the same way and the second hit is mirrored back. You have to alternate. *Link: it's a copy that learns, like everything the programme makes.*
- **Clues planted:**
  - The frame holds no photo, only a corner stamp: *"BD-CP · 04 · DAY 1"* (tank four).
  - The comic's first page shows the Synthetic fight *before it happens* (the comic is a log).
- **Mechanics introduced:** investigation; first upgrade trace (Mimic); comic page 1 of 15 is in the lair.
- **Hook:** the comic flips itself to the next panel: Thanos's pod, open. In the lair, Thanos's pod hisses.

**2. Snapped** (lair → Thanos's pod fight → the snap in deep space)
- **Steps:** fight Thanos; get snapped into deep space; beat Ebony Maw; snap everyone back.
- **Boss rule, Thanos:** the Gauntlet charges toward a snap at 50% HP. The stone that sparks green is a Doombot core. Hit it during the wind-up to stagger him; miss it and you're snapped. *Link: Doom built the Gauntlet.*
- **Clue planted:** green sparks on the Gauntlet (Doombot, 1 of 3).
- **Hook:** Ebony Maw's last words: "It was a gift. From a man in green armour." The sky cracks and a second Earth hangs over Bangalore.

**3. Incursion** (roof → Multiverse Gate → Final Incursion → Convergence)
- **Steps:**
  1. Read the multiverse map in the lair. Investigation: 3 Continuity files on other worlds through the Gate, showing the pattern of Earths pulled close and emptied.
  2. The incoming Earth's heroes are split over letting it through. Play Civil War; AvX and Injustice are optional, each worth a comic page.
  3. Stop the Final Incursion.
  4. Meet the other Earth's {N}. **Choice 1.**
  5. Doom steps through. Galactus follows (Convergence): destroy the siphons, and Galactus eats "Doom".
- **Boss rule, Galactus:** he's untouchable until all four siphons are down (existing mechanic). "Doom" can't be hurt at all. *Link: Doom isn't here to fight; he's showing Galactus the way.*
- **Choice 1: "Let them through?"**
  - **Pull the other {N} through the breach.** Your Earth takes more damage; wreckage stays in the street.
  - **Seal the breach.** Their Earth falls.
  - Consequences: if pulled through, that {N} vanishes that night (harvested) and is found in a tank in ch 14. If sealed, a grave-marker for "the other one" appears by the lake in ch 9. The Bugle headline differs, and Doom quotes the choice in ch 15.
- **Clues planted:**
  - Green sparks on Doom's shoulder (Doombot, 2 of 3).
  - After Galactus eats him: a crater of circuitry that stays in the street (Doombot, 3 of 3).
- **World change:** Doombot debris in the street (persists); the Galactus helmet on the skyline (existing trophy).
- **Hook:** the sky goes white. The Beyonder: "Something older has noticed."

**4. Beyond** (roof → Final Incursion vs the Beyonder)
- **Steps:** go up to the roof; beat the Beyonder.
- **Boss rule, the Beyonder:** he only becomes solid while he is *warning* you. When his lines appear, stop attacking and stand still to listen; he then drops his guard for 4 seconds. Attacking through the warning makes him intangible. *Link: nobody listens.*
- **Story beat:** the warning is about "the man in green who did not die, and the man under the tower who keeps the book." He tears a handful of pages out of the lair comic and scatters them across every zone. This is the in-story source of the collectible pages.
- **Change:** losing no longer ends the story in the Void. You retry. The Void and First Spark stay as free-play content.
- **Hook:** morning. The weapon rack is empty, and a drone with a *BROKER DYNAMICS* logo hovers outside your window. The Bugle has a photo of "a man in a cheap mask" at the incursion site.

### Act 2: The Broker's City

**5. The Broker** (roof → street tail → lake warehouse → tower auction → tower roof)
- **Steps:**
  1. Shoot down the drones.
  2. Tail the truck (existing).
  3. Infiltrate the lake warehouse (existing). Investigation: 3 files: the auction lot list, which includes your stolen weapons (the Overfit took them) and "B3-U6 (unfinished)", i.e. Echo; a Latverian wire transfer; a door sign reading *"SUBLEVEL: DO NOT OPEN"*.
  4. Fight up the tower floors to the auction.
  5. The Broker on his roof.
  6. **Choice 2.**
- **Boss rule, the Broker:** he scans you between attacks. A green scan line sweeps the roof, and if it touches you he gets a damage buff ("I know your numbers"). Crouch behind cover to break line of sight. Armour pieces still break at thresholds. *Link: he's measuring his product.*
- **Clue planted:** mid-fight, the Broker says "Come on, number four." (Tank four, 2 of 3.)
- **Choice 2: "Take his file, or take him in?"**
  - **Take the file.** You read "Subject 04: adaptive" and don't understand it. The Broker walks.
  - **Take him in.** The police take him; no file.
  - Consequences: the file becomes a readable lair file and its line recurs in ch 14. If he walked, his Last Stand exo-suit is upgraded (ch 9 is harder). Doom quotes the choice in ch 15.
- **Hook:** whichever way it goes, the tower's servers upload everything at once, and every screen in Bangalore shows Ultron.

**6. Ultron** (roof → street → substations and servers → data centre)
- **Steps:**
  1. Drones in the street.
  2. Cut the connections. The servers now hold the programme scan archive (investigation: 2 files, "SCAN 04" and "SCAN B3-U6").
  3. Ultron's scrap body at the data centre.
- **Boss rule, Ultron:** he can't be hurt while any cable stands (existing). New: each cable is labelled with a body scan, one of them yours. *Link: he's made of the programme's data.*
- **Folded in:** the Synthetic's server fight (old y2); the Overfit as the model running on his servers.
- **Clue planted:** a Bugle headline: *"STRANGE GREEN DEBRIS IN OLD STREET: SCIENTISTS BAFFLED"*.
- **Hook:** unplugged, Ultron's last broadcast: "Scans… sold… one of their copies walks your lair." Down in the lair, every pod is open.

**7. The Puppeteer** (lair → street → market → theatre)
- **Steps:**
  1. The pods are empty.
  2. Free the possessed villains in the street.
  3. The market. Investigation: find the 3 real people among her fakes (old Synthetic y1).
  4. The heroes (Gwen, Carter, Jay).
  5. The theatre: she replays your old fights, slightly wrong (old Synthetic y3), then the boss.
- **Boss rule, the Puppeteer:** she's shielded by puppets until they're freed (existing). New: her strings *also* catch {N} for 2 seconds (reversed controls), and she's puzzled by it: "Funny. You shouldn't be on my strings." *Link: she can only control copies.* This is an extra tank-four clue.
- **Hook:** her last words: "They threw me away too. The next reject is already wearing your face." The Bugle fills with clips of someone copying you, badly.

**8. Copycat** (lair Bugle → market chase → tower roof in the rain)
- **Steps:** read the Bugle; clean up his mess in the market; the chase; the rooftop fight; the "last page" ending, where you wake in your room.
- **Boss rule, the Copycat:** he copies your last move (existing).
- **Choice 3: "Unmask him, or hand him over quietly?"**
  - **Hand him over.** Bond with the Copycat starts high; he trusts you in Act 3.
  - **Unmask him for the cameras.** His bond starts low, his lines are colder, and there's a Bugle front page about it.
- **Clue:** first use of *"Same time, next story."* Here it's a smug taunt from a small-time crook.
- **World change:** the Copycat's mask on your floor (existing prop); the tower lights come on for good.
- **Hook:** the room scene (waking up in daylight: "It was just a story. Probably."), then the comic closes itself. Years later, the sky over Bangalore turns orange.

### Act 3: Legacy

**9. Last Stand** (roof → street → market → tower → the dam)
- **Steps:**
  1. Fires everywhere.
  2. The Broker's men in your street.
  3. The market on fire.
  4. The Broker at his tower.
  5. The dam.
- **New pressure:** the countdown clock (old Deadline HUD) is now the dam's failure timer, and it runs through the chapter.
- **Investigation:** at the tower, find the dam camera footage. The clone's timestamp label reads *"T4-B"*. (Tank four, 3 of 3.)
- **Boss rule, your clone:** it mirrors you exactly. Neither of you can win. The fight turns into a last stand at low HP, and you hold it at the dam until the timer ends.
- **Story beat:** {N}'s one real death. The dam holds; you don't. The flood breaches the vault under the tower, and **Patient Zero gets out** (shown in one post-death panel).
- **World change:** {N}'s grave at the lake (persists); the Copycat leaves your claws on it.
- **Hook:** five years on. The screens change: someone is selling copies of {N}.

**10. Echo** (grave → street → data centre → theatre → tower roof; *play as the Copycat*)
- **Steps:**
  1. Take the claws from the grave.
  2. Clones two to five (batch three), with the existing clone rules: fast, armoured, a pair.
  3. The sixth clone on the roof believes it's {N}. Stop it without killing it.
  4. At the grave, show it the truth.
  5. The Replicator.
- **Companions:** Echo joins. The bond system starts here: Tab switching and Echo's guard ability.
- **Boss rule, the Replicator:** break its cores before it reprints the clones (existing n3).
- **Clue:** Echo's file (at the grave scene): *"B3-U6 · template: SUBJECT 04 · base: PATIENT ZERO"*.
- **"Same time, next story":** now a promise between the Copycat and Echo.
- **Hook:** the existing cliffhanger. Someone on the tower roof turns around. It's {N}: "Did you miss me?" The Broker regrew him from tank four, and {N} believes he survived. Echo feels something under the tower wake up.

**11. Patient Zero** (tower: basement vault → gym → offices → sky garden)
- **Steps:**
  1. The vault, opened from the inside. Log files explain batch zero to five.
  2. The gym (his failed copies learn).
  3. The offices (copies of the three of you).
  4. The sky garden.
- **Boss rule, Patient Zero:** he adapts to whoever hits him most, so switch between the three of you (existing). New: every upgrade you've bought is one he has too. He says so: "You take from them. Like I do. Where do you think you got that?" *Link: foreshadows the reveal.*
- **Unwinnable:** at 36% HP he stops adapting. Echo steps in and dies. Echo's death scene depends on bond (§5.5). He blasts {N} and the Copycat into space.
- **Folded in:** the Scrambler's "copy it back" becomes the Copycat's companion ability.
- **Hook:** "TO BE CONTINUED." Stars.

**12. The Original** (deep space → home → tower lobby → street → market → Broker Tower roof)
- **Steps:**
  1. The crash rock.
  2. Thanos's wreck. Investigation: the Gauntlet socket with a burnt-out Doombot core, and a Latverian comm log in which the Broker and Doom discuss "specimen four's adaptation". This is the first time the deal is stated outright, and only in part.
  3. The Overfit guarding the gate. His rule: he memorises your last three moves, so vary them.
  4. Fall home to the lake.
  5. Patient Zero's file in the lobby (eleven murders).
  6. The street (buildings fall; existing wreck system).
  7. The market: the Copycat dies. His death scene depends on bond.
  8. The roof: knock him off.
- **Boss rule, Patient Zero (final):** at 30% he can't be hurt any more; drive him off the edge (existing).
- **"Same time, next story":** the Copycat says it as a goodbye.
- **World change:** city wreckage (persists, no longer cleared); a second and third grave by the lake.
- **Hook:** a Broker Dynamics truck collects Patient Zero's body from the street. A week later, at the lake, a text: "Same time, next story. I kept the backups."

### Act 4: The Last Page

**13. Backups** (lake → tower lobby → clone vault 1–3)
- **Steps:**
  1. Flowers on the graves.
  2. The hidden lift.
  3. Vault levels 1 and 2 (security clones of old villains).
  4. The bottom level: the Broker's offer. **Choice 4.**
- **Investigation:** on each vault level, find the batch manifest page. Tanks 1–3 are empty. Tank 4 is labelled *"RELEASED (x2)"* and the player can read it, but no one comments.
- **Choice 4: "Work for him?"**
  - **Refuse.** Smash the console; the tanks drain (existing).
  - **Accept.** One extra mission: carry a data core to the tower roof, where a Latverian drone collects it. Then the Broker wakes them anyway.
  - Consequences: if you accepted, Doom has your core in ch 15 ("You worked for us. For one night."), and ch 14's Broker dialogue changes.
- **Hook:** "TO BE CONTINUED." The tanks drain.

**14. Batch Five** (vault → gym → offices → sky garden → the walk out)
- **Steps:**
  1. Echo and the Copycat wake, hollow (existing).
  2. Fight them under the kill switch. Blocking doesn't help; with high bond their hits are weaker ("they're holding back").
  3. The climb, with the lights failing.
  4. **The tank-four reveal** at the sky garden. The Broker lists the clues back: the empty photo, "number four", *T4-B*, the strings, the upgrades.
  5. The Broker in the Patient Zero suit.
  6. The walk out; a new copy steps out of a tank.
- **Boss rule, the Broker in the Patient Zero suit:** the suit absorbs the upgrades you bought. Each absorbed one is disabled for you until you break the matching glowing plate on the suit. *Link: it's Patient Zero's power in the Broker's hands.*
- **Choice consequence:** if Choice 1 was "pulled through", the other {N} is in a tank on the bottom level, labelled *"HARVESTED · EARTH-(n)"*.
- **Hook:** the new copy says "Where it all starts." Final card: "The first thing he did was ask for his name." Then a green light falls out of the sky into your street.

**15. Doom** (street → market → data centre → Broker Tower roof)
- **Steps:**
  1. The Doombot wreck. Its circuitry matches the ch 3 crater.
  2. Doombots in the market.
  3. The signal at the data centre (Ultron's old servers). Investigation: the harvest ledger, a count of every {N} taken.
  4. Doom on the roof.
- **Boss rule, Doom:** four Dooms on the roof. Three are Doombots and spark green; the real one doesn't. Only hits on the real Doom count. Hitting a Doombot shuffles them. *Link: every Doombot clue pays off here.*
- **Finale:**
  - Doom quotes all four choices.
  - He explains the deal plainly: he wanted the one version that couldn't be copied, "and you were a copy all along".
  - The incursions close in (existing ending).
  - The final memory shows whoever the player bonded with (§5.5).
  - The multiverse ends.
- **Page 1 card:** if all 15 comic pages are collected, one extra card before the credits: page 1 of the log, dated before chapter 1, showing the handshake between Doom and the Broker.
- **Credits:** the only credits roll in the story (silent).

## 5. Mechanics

All new state lives in the existing `SG` save object (`ru-story`), with a version marker `SG.v = 2`.

### 5.1 Choices (4)

- **Data:** `SG.choice = { breach, file, mask, deal }`, each set to one of its two values or left unset.
- **When they happen:** a choice scene pauses the story with two buttons, styled like the dialogue box. The choice is only final once the scene ends.
- **Recorded in the lair comic:** the comic stand gets a "Log" panel. Each choice is a panel captioned in the Broker's voice, e.g. *"Subject 04 sealed the breach. Note: sentimental about self."*
- **Consequences:**

  | Choice | Shows up in | Also changes |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1. breach | ch 14 tank | Bugle, lake marker |
  | 2. file | ch 9 difficulty, ch 14 line | Lair files |
  | 3. mask | Bond start, lines | Bugle |
  | 4. deal | ch 14 and 15 lines | — |

- **Finale:** Doom quotes all four.

**Story tie:** the log is the Broker's; he's recording how his product decides.

### 5.2 Upgrades ("traces")

- **Earning traces:** beating a *copy* (the Synthetic, clones, puppets, security clones, hollow companions, the Copycat, Echo's fight) grants a trace named after what it could do.
- **Spending:** traces are spent at a new bench in the lair (next to the weapon rack) on 5 upgrades, 3 levels each:

  | Upgrade | Effect |
  |---|---|
  | Mimic | Copies the last enemy special |
  | Mend | Regeneration |
  | Iron | Damage taken reduced |
  | Edge | Damage dealt up |
  | Reflex | Dash / guard stamina |

- **Replaces:** the per-chapter stat tweaks (old-body HP cuts, frailty multipliers) are removed. The hero's strength now comes from what the player bought.
- **Safety net:** Last Stand still forces low HP by story, and a player with no upgrades can still finish every chapter.

**Story tie:** absorbing abilities from copies is exactly what Patient Zero does. He says so in ch 11, and in ch 14 the Broker's suit takes the upgrades back.

### 5.3 Investigation

- **Where:** chapters 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 12, 13 and 15 have a step that needs N files or clues found in the area before the fight site unlocks.
- **How it looks:** files are glowing folders or objects. The beacon points to the nearest unfound one, and a counter appears in the goal line ("Files found 2/3").
- **Reading them:** each file opens a short readable card and is kept in the lair "Files" panel.
- **What they add up to:** read in order, they tell the programme's history.

**Story tie:** they're Continuity Programme paperwork.

### 5.4 One rule per boss

- Every boss has one rule (listed per chapter above). It's taught through a NARR beat at the start of the fight and a hint if the player breaks it twice.
- Each rule reflects that villain's link to the programme (copying, scanning, strings, adapting, absorbing, decoys).

### 5.5 Companion bond (Echo, the Copycat)

- **Data:** `SG.bond = { echo: 0–100, copy: 0–100 }`. The Copycat starts at 60 or 30 depending on Choice 3; Echo starts at 50.
- **Gaining bond:** the companion lands the finishing blow; you revive or switch in to protect a downed companion; optional lair conversations between chapters (existing `lairTalk`); and a few dialogue lines where the player can answer kindly or not.
- **Abilities:** Echo's guard break and the Copycat's "copy it back" get stronger with bond.
- **Death scenes:**

  | Bond | Echo (ch 11) | Copycat (ch 12) |
  |---|---|---|
  | ≥ 70 | A personal last line and a named memory | Same |
  | ≤ 30 | Short and cold | Same |

  In ch 14, at high bond, the hollow copies hold back for one moment.
- **Final memory (ch 15):** whoever is ≥ 70 appears. Both, one, or nobody: "You were alone for most of it."

### 5.6 Comic pages (15)

- **Placement:** one page per chapter, hidden in that chapter's zone (room, lair, roof, deep space, each Bangalore district, tower floors, vault, space rocks).
- **Collecting:** pages stay collectable in free play after their chapter, and a lair "Pages" panel shows the collected panels.
- **What they show:** together, the true timeline of the deal, from the handshake to "tank four, day one".
- **Reward:** collecting all 15 adds the page 1 card at the end.

**Story tie:** the Beyonder tore them out of the Broker's log in ch 4.

### 5.7 Persistent world

- **Stays once it appears:** Doombot debris in the street (ch 3); the Galactus helmet (existing); tower lights on (ch 8); graves at the lake (ch 9, 12, and a marker for Choice 1); city wreckage (ch 12, no longer cleared).
- **Lair trophies:** one per boss, accumulating on the lair shelves (Gauntlet shard, Ultron's eye, the strings, the mask, and so on).
- **Bugle:** headlines read `SG.choice` and the chapter reached.

## 6. Presentation

- **Acts:** `BOOKS` becomes 4 acts.
  - **Chapter cards:** "Act N · Chapter M".
  - **Story menu:** grouped by act.
  - **Between acts:** an end-of-act card (the existing `bookEnd` pattern).
- **Cutscenes:** every one runs through `cinStart` or `cardSeq`, so it's skippable.
- **Goal hints and nav beacons:** they cover every zone, including investigation targets.
- **Credits:** one roll, after ch 15.
- **Free play is untouched:** event lobbies, pods, the Gate, the Void, Galactus rematches, and trophies from free play.
