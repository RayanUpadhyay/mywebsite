# Saltwater: design doc

Status: **in progress.** Chapter 1 is built; one chapter a day after that.

- **Site:** `saltwater.rayanupadhyay.com`.
- **Where the code goes:** its own repo and Vercel project, like Unrendered. This repo only holds this design doc. Saltwater is not part of `index.html`.
- **What it is:** a first-person flood survival game on the rayanupadhyay.com map: build a raft, scavenge, fight sharks and bandits, and drain the city.

---

## Canon

- **When:** years before the collapse that Unrendered starts thirty years after. The city is the bright one from rayanupadhyay.com, drowned.
- **Characters:** all new. No characters from the main site appear.
- **Small references (only two):**
  - a late-night radio DJ who says "listen to the static", never named (Deacon Hale);
  - after the credits, grey static behind the sealed barrier at the city's edge.

## The hook

One night the barrier at the edge of the city map breaks and the sea pours into Bangalore. By morning the streets are underwater; rooftops, treetops, the Purple Line viaduct and the cricket ground's floodlights stick out of the sea. The sea brought sharks. The flood brought bandits.

You wake on a rooftop with nothing, build a raft, survive, and work your way to the edge of the city to close the breach. When it's sealed, the sea goes back out and the map is dry again.

## Survival

- **Thirst and hunger:** catch rain, distil seawater, fish, and scavenge tins from flooded flats.
- **The raft:** starts as a door and two barrels and grows with planks, a sail, a shelter, a still, a stove and a lookout.
- **Diving:** limited breath; the best salvage is deepest.
- **Crafting:** a spear, a harpoon gun, a machete, armour made from road signs, and later a raft cannon.
- **Day and night:** bandits raid at night, sharks hunt all the time, and storms can wreck an untied raft.

## What attacks you

- **Sharks:**
  - Reef sharks circle divers.
  - Bull sharks ram the raft.
  - **Old Grey**, a huge great white, hunts you all game.
- **Bandits:**
  - The Scavs, small raiders.
  - The Tolls, who rope off waterways and charge to pass.
  - Captain Rook's Fleet, a floating fortress.
- **Other dangers:** storms, currents and collapsing buildings, plus crocodiles out of the zoo, and eels and jellyfish in the deep.

## The map

The OG city underwater, on the main site's grid: roads every 55 m, the Purple Line along the z = −55 road, the market, the parks, the temple and the cricket ground.

The flood adds:
- the **market roofs**, a floating trading post and the hub;
- the **Wreck Field** of stranded ships;
- **Rook's Fortress**;
- the flooded metro tunnels;
- **the Breach** at the edge of the map.

## Characters

- **Cass Rowan, 19:** a lifeguard at the city pool, and you.
- **Old Moss, 70:** a retired fisherman who runs the trading post.
- **Lux Ferrer, 16:** a jet-ski scout.
- **Dr. Imogen Hale-Price, 44:** an engineer who worked on the barrier.
- **Captain Rook:** the bandit king, who wants the city to stay drowned.

## Chapters

1. **Washed Up:** wake on a rooftop. Make a spear, catch rain, dive for food, build a raft from a door and two barrels, fight off a reef shark, and reach the smoke on the market roofs.
2. **The Market Roofs:** the trading post and Old Moss. Trading, crafting and fishing open up. **Boss:** a bull shark.
3. **The Wreck Field:** board stranded ships held by the Scavs. **Boss:** the Scavs' leader. **Hook:** Dr. Hale-Price's broadcast.
4. **The Viaduct:** fight the Tolls along the Purple Line, with a jet-ski chase and a barge fight. **Boss:** the Toll boss. **Small reference:** the radio DJ.
5. **The Stadium:** dive through the flooded cricket ground. **Boss:** Old Grey, first fight; you survive, you don't win.
6. **Rook's Fortress:** free Dr. Hale-Price. **Boss:** Captain Rook, first fight; he escapes.
7. **The Storm:** cross open water in a storm with Rook's fleet chasing. **Boss:** Captain Rook.
8. **The Breach:** the final dive to seal the barrier. **Final boss:** Old Grey.

## The ending

With the breach sealed, the sea drains out over three days. The last scene is Cass and Lux walking down MG Road on dry ground, past sand, seaweed and stranded boats, with a shark tooth stuck in a lamppost. After the credits: behind the sealed barrier there's no sea at all, just grey static.
