# rayanupadhyay.com

My personal website, live at **[rayanupadhyay.com](https://rayanupadhyay.com)**.

Instead of a normal portfolio page, the site is a 3D room you can walk around in. The desk, laptop, bookshelf and music corner are how you find my research, projects and music, and the room opens out into a whole world of side stories and mini-games.

## What's inside

- **The room:** a first-person 3D bedroom built with Three.js. Walk with WASD or the on-screen joystick, look around, and click objects to open them. There's a guided tour for first-time visitors.
- **Research:** my two published papers, *[Fairness and Dependency Preservation in Synthetic Credit Data](https://nhsjs.com/2026/fairness-and-dependency-preservation-in-synthetic-credit-data/)* (NHSJS, 2026) and *[Emotion-associated representations in the feed forward gates of a language model](https://doi.org/10.64336/001c.175619)* (Journal of High School Science, 10(4), 2026). Both have interactive versions on [research.rayanupadhyay.com](https://research.rayanupadhyay.com).
- **Second paper:** *[Emotion-associated representations in the feed forward gates of a language model](https://jhss.scholasticahq.com/article/175619-emotion-associated-representations-in-the-feed-forward-gates-of-a-language-model)*, Journal of High School Science, 10(4), 2026 ([doi:10.64336/001c.175619](https://doi.org/10.64336/001c.175619)).
- **Projects:** [Mandalink](https://mandalink.org), my Mandarin radicals app; AgriBridge, an Android app for farmers in India, which I presented at the 64th UN Commission for Social Development (the APK is in `downloads/`); and [MEDCON 2026](https://medcon.tisb.org).
- **Little sites:** small side projects on their own subdomains, linked from the Sites button, the laptop and the simple view: [tools](https://tools.rayanupadhyay.com), [study](https://study.rayanupadhyay.com), [links](https://links.rayanupadhyay.com), [agribridge](https://agribridge.rayanupadhyay.com) and [research](https://research.rayanupadhyay.com).
- **Music:** live charts from my Last.fm, a crate of my vinyl and speakers that play lo-fi.
- **News pill:** a short announcement driven by `news.json`, plus a news feed pulled from Mandalink's announcement board.
- **The city:** step out of the room into an island version of Bangalore, cut off from the other cities and the Far Range mountains by the sea. You can walk, drive, take the ferry or swim. Your tower has an underground garage with about ten vehicles to take out.
- **Seasons:** a new story every four weeks, starting 18 November 2026 (see below).
- **Mandalink story and mini-games:** a Mandalink world of radical islands, side quests and a growing list of arcade games.
- **Private channel:** the walkie-talkie on the desk (or `/#chat`) opens an end-to-end encrypted, password-protected chat. See below.
- **Simple view** for anyone who just wants the information without the 3D, and a printable layout.

## Seasons

The world runs in seasons of four weeks each, with a new part unlocking every week. Each season has its own villain, boss fights, a tool or vehicle you keep, three skins to earn and a change it leaves on the map for good. The phone's Season tab shows your progress, lets you replay the season's scenes, and holds your Loadout and Wardrobe.

### Season 1 · Landfall (18 November – 15 December 2026)

A meteor crashes into the sea off the west coast, and the Salvager wants every piece of it.

| Part | Unlocks | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Impact | 18 Nov | The meteor comes down. The Keeper gives you a scanner at the ferry pier; find three fragments on the beaches, then fight off a scrapper ambush on the west beach. |
| 2 · Salvage | 25 Nov | The Salvager's drones race you to four more fragments. Take the ferry to his barge and beat the Hauler, an armoured rig you have to get behind. |
| 3 · Crash Isle | 2 Dec | A new island rises where the meteor fell. Find three crystal shards past the searchlights, beat the Foreman (shielded by crystal pylons) and light the core. |
| 4 · The Salvager | 9 Dec | The finale, in three phases: his drone swarm and magnet, his salvage mech, then him while the crater floods. |

- **Weapons you keep:** blaster pistol, scatter gun, scrap rifle, shard sword, heavy wrench and harpoon spear. You carry one gun and one melee weapon (Q switches; phones get Fire, Swing and Switch buttons).
- **Skins:** Crater Scout (light the core), Meteor Glow (beat the Foreman without being spotted) and Salvager's Rig (beat the finale).
- **Left on the map:** Crash Isle, with the core's beam.
- Every boss has an intro and an outro scene, and there's a Season 1 trailer in the Season tab.

### Season 2 · High Water (16 December 2026 – 12 January 2027)

A wave pours through the tear and drowns Bangalore. You wake up on a plank in a flooded city you can swim anywhere, with sharks in the water and Captain Brine's pirates on the rooftops.

| Part | Unlocks | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Adrift | 16 Dec | Find a boat, reach the survivors' rooftop camp, get a harpoon gun, rescue three people and survive the great shark. |
| 2 · Brine's Harbour | 23 Dec | Raiders steal the camp's supplies. Catch their boats, then beat First Mate Sable. |
| 3 · The Deep | 30 Dec | Dive to the drowned pump house, turn its three valves, and beat Ironjaw. |
| 4 · High Water | 6 Jan | Board Captain Brine's ship as the pumps start, and the city drains. |

- **Kept:** the boat, the harpoon gun and the diving gear.
- **Skins:** Diver, Canal Captain (finish Part 1 without being bitten) and Brine's Coat.
- **Left on the map:** the sunken district, with Brine's wreck.

### Season 3 · The Syndicate (13 January – 9 February 2027)

When the water drains, the Marigold Syndicate owns half the city: every shop pays, and their stamp is on every door. Inspector Reyes, whose station is still full of mud, asks you to help.

| Part | Unlocks | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Protection | 13 Jan | Break the rackets on your street, chase a collector through the market (vault the barricades, slide under the shutters), survive the night raid, and beat Brass, the enforcer. |
| 2 · The Ledger | 20 Jan | Rob the Syndicate's counting house (quiet is optional: sight cones, takedowns, flashbangs), beat the Ledger and find out who he really is. Then a fight you can't win. |
| 3 · The Velvet Run | 27 Jan | Get a rope launcher and the Comet. Stop three convoys by dropping on them from a roof or spiking their tyres; one is a decoy. Then run Velvet down. |
| 4 · Marigold | 3 Feb | Storm Marigold's tower: the lobby, a swing route up the outside past the snipers, a rooftop chase, and Marigold in her greenhouse. |

- **Five districts** to free (three of each one's four rackets), and **heat**: with two marigolds or more the Syndicate ambushes you in alleys, from vans and with snipers. Lie low or hide in your garage to cool off.
- **New moves:** slide (sprint, then crouch) and vault (sprint at a low wall), from the start of the season.
- **Kept:** the rope launcher, the Comet (nitro and tyre spikes; it waits in your garage) and the stun baton.
- **Skins:** Undercover (finish the story), Clean Hands (do the heist unseen) and Marigold's Suit (beat the finale).
- **Left on the map:** Marigold's tower, dark now and Reyes's, and marigold graffiti across the city.

Add `?week=N` to the address to preview a later part, or `?season=N` to preview another season.

## Tech

The whole site is a single self-contained **`index.html`** (apart from one small script per season): vanilla JavaScript, CSS, and [Three.js r128](https://threejs.org/) from cdnjs. There's no build step and no framework. It's hosted on GitHub Pages with a custom domain (`CNAME`).

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The entire site |
| `seasons/` | One script per season (`s01.js` is Season 1), loaded only while that season runs |
| `news.json` | The current news-pill message, link and expiry date |
| `og-image.png` | Social preview image |
| `downloads/` | Files offered for download (AgriBridge APK) |
| `CNAME` | Custom domain for GitHub Pages |
| `CHAT_SETUP.md` | How to set up the private chat |
| `CLAUDE.md` | Notes on how the code fits together |
| `SEASON_PLANS.md` | Plans for seasons that aren't built yet |

## Running locally

Any static file server works:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then open http://localhost:8000
```

Opening `index.html` directly mostly works too, but some features (like loading `news.json`) need it served over HTTP.

## Updating the news pill

Edit `news.json`:

```json
{
  "id": "open-house-2026",
  "message": "🚪 Free Open House badge on Mandalink, celebrating the launch of rayanupadhyay.com",
  "link": "https://mandalink.org",
  "linkText": "Claim it",
  "until": "2026-12-07"
}
```

Give each new message a new `id`. The pill stops showing after the `until` date.

## Private chat

Messages are encrypted in the browser with AES-GCM using a key derived from the room password (PBKDF2, 250,000 rounds), then stored in Supabase as ciphertext. Nobody without the password can read them, including Supabase. A wrong password just opens a different, empty room. Setup takes about five minutes and is described in [`CHAT_SETUP.md`](CHAT_SETUP.md); use a long password, since the ciphertext is publicly readable.

## Secrets

You found the repo, so you deserve a gift for checking it out. A few cheat codes:

1. Open the laptop in the bedroom and go to the Terminal.
2. Type `sudo wardrobe --all` (or `iddqd`) to unlock every season skin.
3. Type `sudo unlock --forever` (or `kaleidoscope`) to unlock every suit, every suit variant and every weapon skin for good, including any that later seasons add.
4. Type `sudo money --infinite` (or `motherlode`) for infinite cash. Run it again to turn it off.
5. Type `sudo travel --secret` (or `xyzzy`) to visit Lanternfall, a city that isn't on any map. Walk back through the glowing gate to get home.

None of them show up in `help`. Keep it between us.
