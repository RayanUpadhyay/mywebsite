# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## What this is

Rayan Upadhyay's personal site (rayanupadhyay.com), hosted on GitHub Pages (`CNAME`). The **entire site is one self-contained `index.html`** (~20k lines, ~2.6 MB): inline CSS, inline vanilla JS and Three.js **r128** from cdnjs (post-processing extras load lazily from `cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.128.0/examples/js/`). There is no build step, no framework, no package.json and no test suite. Keep it that way: everything goes inside `index.html`, and Three.js stays on r128.

The `*.rayanupadhyay.com` "little sites" (tools, study, wish, links, agribridge, research) live in **separate repos/Vercel projects**. This repo only links to them (Sites section, RayanOS, terminal `open` list, README).

## Running and checking

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000      # serve locally; news.json needs HTTP, not file://
```

There are no tests. Before committing an edit to `index.html`, at least syntax-check every inline script:

```bash
python3 - <<'EOF'
import re
s = open('index.html', encoding='utf-8').read()
js = re.findall(r'<script(?![^>]*src)[^>]*>(.*?)</script>', s, re.S)
open('/tmp/chk.js', 'w').write('\n;\n'.join('(function(){' + x + '\n})' for x in js))
EOF
node --check /tmp/chk.js
```

For behaviour checks, drive the page headlessly with Playwright/Chromium. Click `#simpleBtn` for the simple view; the 3D room needs WebGL (`--use-gl=swiftshader`). Check phone width (390px) as well as desktop, plus light and dark themes.

## Architecture (the big picture)

- **Two views over the same content.** `#simple` (the "Simple version") holds the real content as `<section class="sec" data-s="KEY">` blocks (about, research, projects, sites, music, vinyl, writing, news, highlights, contact, vault, plus `only-panel` ones like swim and photos). Those sections are the **single source of truth**:
  - `openPanel(key)` copies a section's `innerHTML` into the room's side panel when you click an object or a dock button (`data-go="KEY"`).
  - `rosApp(k)` (RayanOS, the laptop desktop) copies sections into its windows, e.g. `sites`, `music` and `news`.
  - So when you change content, change the section, and don't rely on DOM injected into a section: it gets copied too. Visibility tweaks for the simple view belong in CSS scoped to `#simple .in>.sec` (not `.simple`: the "At a glance" sheet `#brief` reuses the `.simple` classes).
- **Simple view layout.** The simple view opens on a bento grid (`.bento` / `.bt` tiles). Each tile's `[data-more="KEY"]` opens one full section under the grid at a time (`bOpen`), and `.bback` ("↑ Top") closes it. In the simple view the little sites are opt-in only, behind the Sites tile and the `.xtoggle` button. RayanOS removes that toggle and shows them all.
- **The 3D world.** It is one big async IIFE: the room (WASD/joystick, click objects; clicking or tapping the floor no longer walks you anywhere: `pick` doesn't return floor hits any more, and only the farm still uses `walkTarget`, to walk you up to a tile or animal you clicked), then the city (an island: beach, sea, ferry and swimming to the other cities and to the Far Range, a mountain land you can climb; its zone `mtn` handles terrain height in `mtnStep`, because the movement code is frozen), side quests, mini-games and other worlds (`enterWorld`/`exitWorld`, `travel(dest)`). Dialogue uses `say()` and achievements use `secret()`. If WebGL/THREE fails, the page falls back to the simple view.
- **Stories: only the Mandalink one is left.** The old story modes (the Multiverse Saga comic, the Books, Meridian General with Code Black and System Failure, Kiran and the stories after the comic, The Tidy Man, the Hollow, and BERSERKER with its space worlds) were removed to make way for a seasons system. Code that still mentions them goes through small stand-ins in blocks headed `retired: …` (they report the stories as never started); don't build on those, and remove a stand-in together with its last caller. Their saved progress is cleared by the `retired stories` block. The Mandalink island story (猴王闹海岛) stays, and the phone's Story tab (`storyStarted()`) only shows once it has started.
- **Seasons.** 15 loosely linked seasons, four weeks each, starting 18 November 2026 and cycling (`seasonInfo()` in `index.html`; `?season=N` previews one). Each has its own villain, a tool or vehicle the player keeps, a permanent trace on the map, three skins and four weekly Parts (the in-game word; older notes say chapters). A season's content lives in `seasons/sNN.js`, loaded only while that season runs (with `?v=SEASON_V`; bump `SEASON_V` in `index.html` whenever a season file changes, so browsers don't reuse an old copy); it defines `window.RU_SEASON_N = R => ({ step, tab, wire })` and gets everything it needs from the page through the API object `R` (`seasonApi()`), never by reaching into page internals. That is the one exception to "everything goes inside `index.html`" (season files, plus `seasons/s01-trailer.mp4`, which only downloads when someone presses play). After a season ends its file is loaded again with `{ past: true }` and only builds what it left on the map (Crash Isle with its lit core and ferry stop, for Season 1); list every season that has a file in `SEASON_FILES`. Progress is saved under `ru-season` (one key per season, e.g. `s1`), and the player's name under `ru-heroname` (16 characters, cleaned by `cleanName()`, default "Traveller"). The phone has a Season tab. All villains, aliens and skins are original (no Marvel or DC characters). Real winter (December to early January) stays a bonus layer on top of whatever season is running.

Each has an intro and an outro scene (`scene(id, shots, done)`, `SCN`): they play once and can be replayed from the Season tab, like the Landfall cutscene. The Season tab also plays a Season 1 trailer (`trailer()`, about 45 seconds of the same scene engine). Fights award the season's weapons, and the skins: Crater Scout when the core is lit, Meteor Glow for beating the Foreman without being spotted, Salvager's Rig for the finale. **Fallout** (`FALL`, `fallStart`, `fallStep`, saved as `st.fall`):

  - The smoke group sets `userData.bcn = false`, so the one-waypoint rule doesn't hide it.
  - The Salvager's recovery drone flies in from the sea (faster after Part 2), and the scanner stalls it (`fallJam`). Reach the fragment first for ₹400 (`R.cash`, added to `seasonApi`), or he takes it and says so on the radio. Beating him to five pays a ₹2,000 bonus.
  - The fall pins itself only if you have no other pin. It runs on the real clock, so a slow frame rate doesn't slow it. The Season tab keeps score.

  The Salvager is also in the city from day one, through his "We buy meteor fragments, cash paid, ask for the Salvager" hoardings (`buildPosters`), and the Bulletin posts on Flock after the first fall.
  **Scrapper camps** (`CAMP_DEF`, `campsBuild`, `campStep`, saved as `st.camps`): once the beach ambush is beaten, six small fights wait at road crossings across the city, each guarded by a crew of scrap bots, drones, crew in cover and sometimes a brute, round a pile of crates and glowing scrap. Their `FIGHT['camp_<id>']` objects go through `R.fight`. Walk into the beacon to start one. The rope launcher (`R.pk.rope(true)`, `ropeOn()`) reuses the Bangalore web swing (`webStart`, `SW`, `swingCore`: roof walking, falling off edges) with a rope-coloured line, outside the flood only; `PK.onLand(at, roofHeight, fallSpeed)` hears every landing. - **Bodies and the crowd.** `avBuild` (after `avRealism`) gives avatars a build (`build: { h, w, belly, sh, head }`, on the outfit or on a look) and pieces (`coat`, `kurta`, `saree`, `vest`, `cap`/`peak`, `glasses`, `beard`, `moustache`, `flower`, `sleeveless`, hair `bun`/`ponytail`/`curly`/`side`); the body sits in a scaled inner group with the head scaled back, so `g.scale` is left alone. The loser lies down, bystanders scatter (`pedScatter`) and the police heat goes up.

- **Weapons: inventory, the wheel and carrying in public** (block `the weapon wheel, and carrying weapons in public`, before the season fight code).
  - **Inventory:** `invAll()` is everything you can hold: the lair weapons in `inv` plus the season weapons you own (`ARS.own`, bought at Ironside Arms or won in fights). `hold(id)` takes either; a season weapon (`isSW`, ids like `s1_blaster`) also becomes your loadout pick (`arsEquip`), so you start a season fight with it.   - **Outfit variants** (`SVAR`: Night and Ember for every season skin, registered as outfits `<skin>_night` and `<skin>_ember`): won in `ssFightEnd` while wearing the skin. Night means winning any fight (a season boss, a scrapper camp or a street brawl) between 7 pm and 6 am in game time (`hourNow`). Ember means winning one without losing any health (`EV.tookDmg`, counted in `hurtMe`). `ARS.wvar[skin]` is the pick (`varSet`, `varOf`, `skinOutfit`); `arsLook` and the lair's suit gallery (`lxSuits`) use it.

- **Jail.** Hitting people builds a record (`JAIL.rec`, saved as `ru-record`; a punch adds 1, a knockout 2, and it fades by 1 every 90 seconds). With a record of 3 or more, being caught by the police (`busted`) sends you to a cell; at 8 they come straight after the fight. The cell is an underground level (`LVL.jail`, `jailBuild`, `jailGo`) and there's no sentence: you break out (`jailStep`). Work the loose bar three times (E) while the guard has his back to you (he turns round every 10 to 16 seconds, for under two seconds, `JAIL.look`), squeeze through the gap (`J_GAP` joins the cell and corridor rects), take the keys off his desk and unlock the door. If he sees you out of the cell or at the bar, he puts you back and the bar is fixed (`jailCaught`). After four minutes he falls asleep. Getting out (`jailOut`) puts you back where you were picked up with three heat; the record stays. Or pay bail (`payBail`, the green button or B: ₹500 plus ₹200 per record point) and walk out with the record and heat cleared.

- **Drunk** (`DRUNK.l`, `drink`, `drunkStep`): drinks raise it and food lowers it; it wears off by one unit about every 85 seconds. The camera sways (which also throws your aim off) and blurs (`.drunkfx`), and your steps wobble; Reduce effects tones the sway and blur down. Driving drunk adds police heat every 14 seconds. Past 6 you pass out (`passOut`): you wake a few hours later in game time (`CLOCK.off`, added in `hourNow`) at home or on a park bench, 10 to 25% of your cash gone, with a text from Mum.
- **Season themes (the whole site changes each season, from Season 2).** `SEASON_THEMES` (next to `seasonInfo`) gives each season light and dark colour tokens, body and display fonts (loaded from Google Fonts only while it runs), a CSS grade on the 3D canvas (`#scene`, off with Reduce effects via `html.lowfx`) and the room's accent wall. `seasonTheme(n)` runs at page load and sets `html[data-season]`. - **The story.** `SEASON_PLANS.md` opens with the story bible (the spine, cause and effect between seasons, the mythology and its reveals, character arcs, plants and payoffs, and dialogue rules). Read it before writing any season, and keep its plants-and-payoffs table up to date. Dialogue: no catchphrases or villain monologues, subtext over statement, short lines, and every villain has a reason.
- **The flooded city** (`R.flood`, in `index.html` so later seasons can use it): `floodSet(level)` puts the whole map under water. Validity goes through `floodValid` (buildings above the water are walls, submerged ones you swim over; boats only on open water), and `floodStep` sets your height after movement: on a roof, at the surface, or diving (C or Crouch) with a breath meter (`FLOOD.breathTime`). You climb onto a roof near the water or up a ladder (`floodLadder`) with E. Traffic stops and pedestrians hide. Boats and the plank raft are vehicle types `boat` and `raft` (`FLOAT`). `floodLevel` moves the water; `floodOff` ends it.
- **Season fights, weapons and skins** (in `index.html`, so they outlast the season): `R.fight(o)` (`ssFight`) stages a boss fight in the real streets on top of the street-boss engine (`startEvent('st', …, { part: 'q' + id, site, ss: 1 })`, centred on `o.at` in map coordinates, so the fight works in local coordinates round it). `o` gives `boss` stats and `outfit`, extra `units`, `cover` ([x, z, 'crate' | 'car' | 'none', r]), `setup`, `step(dt, F)`, `hit(u, d, from, F)`, `kill`, `hud`, `goal`, `intro` and `end(won)`; `F` is the helper object (`add`, `mark` = `sbMark`, `hurt`, `near`, `win`, `cover`, `move`, `kill`…). Units with `cover: 1` hide behind cover and lean out to shoot (`ssCover`). The weapons (`SSW`: blaster pistol, scatter gun, scrap rifle, shard sword, heavy wrench, harpoon spear) plug into the fight kit: guns have magazines, reloading (R) and ammo cells; there are hit markers, muzzle flashes and enemies break into scrap. You carry one gun and one melee weapon (Q switches; phones get Fire, Swing and ⇄ buttons, `.sspad`). Owned weapons, the loadout, earned skins (`SSKIN`) and the one you wear live in `ru-arsenal`; the Loadout and Wardrobe cards are appended to the phone's Season tab (`arsTab`), and a worn skin goes over your look (`arsLook`).
